Tuxedoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

Tuxedoku ("the game") is a puzzle game made by Microberry Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is involved when you play Tuxedoku on Android, and the choices you have.

The short version: we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows optional video ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, login or sign-up.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your own device, for example the levels you have completed, hints used, fish earned, items you have unlocked, and your sound and motion settings. This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tuxedoku shows optional rewarded video ads. You only see an ad when you choose to watch one, for example to get an extra hint or to continue a level. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.

To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and general (approximate) location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system and language,
  • information about your interactions with ads.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's own policies. You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Changing your choice: where this applies, you can review or change your consent at any time from the game's Settings screen (Privacy options).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android device settings (usually under Settings, then Privacy or Google, then Ads).
  • Not watching ads: ads are optional. You can play the game without ever choosing to watch one.

5. Analytics

We do not use any analytics or tracking services of our own in the game. If this changes in a future update, we will update this policy first.

6. Children

Tuxedoku is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, we do not store it on any server. Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Since we do not hold personal information about you, most requests are best made to Google for ad-related data. You are also welcome to contact us with any question and we will do our best to help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
